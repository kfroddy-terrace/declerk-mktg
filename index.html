About Us

At DeClerk Property Services, this is more than just yard work — it’s about taking care of people and the places they call home. As a locally owned and operated business here in Central Oregon, we understand the unique climate, seasonal challenges, and pride that comes with maintaining property in this area.

Our focus is simple: provide dependable, honest service and treat every property as if it were our own. Whether it’s routine lawn maintenance, irrigation service, or fire mitigation, we show up on time, communicate clearly, and do the job right.

Many of our clients have trusted us with their properties for years. We value those relationships and work hard to earn that trust through consistency, attention to detail, and genuine care. When you hire DeClerk Property Services, you’re working with someone who takes pride in their work and stands behind it.
